4. Screen Capture and Vision-Mode Media Processing

VRH.AI enables visual context and screenshot capture using chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab:

  • Voluntary Capture: Screenshots are captured exclusively when you explicitly click the tab capture button or initiate an agent task with a vision model selected.
  • Visual Grounding: Viewport captures are annotated with Set-of-Mark markers and sent directly to your configured multimodal AI provider for step reasoning. Older screenshots are automatically pruned from local session memory.

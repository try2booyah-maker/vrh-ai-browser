What are your full capabilities and what can you do?

Hello! I am VRH.AI, your intelligent autonomous browser copilot and virtual assistant running directly inside Google Chrome.

Here are my 9 core platform superpowers:

1. 🌐 Live Web Page Copilot (Ask Mode)

  • Real-time DOM inspection: Reads, parses, and understands the live text, structure, tables, and headings of your active browser tab.
  • In-depth Q&A: Research, fact-checking, and explanations grounded in the active page.

2. 🤖 Autonomous Web Automation (Agent Mode)

  • Perception Engine 2.0 & CDP: Hardware-level browser control via Chrome DevTools Protocol.
  • Set-of-Marks (SoM) visual badges: Interactively clicks [1], [2], types into inputs, submits forms, and scrolls pages.
  • State-Diffing & Safety Gating: Visually verifies page changes and requires human confirmation before payment or checkout actions.

3. 📑 Multi-Tab Context Integration (@)

  • Simultaneously queries and synthesizes information across multiple open browser tabs with automatic token budgeting.

4. 👁️ Multimodal Vision & Screenshots

  • Takes active tab screenshots and inspects attached image files with vision-capable models (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Gemini).

5. 📄 Offline Client-Side PDF & OCR

  • Parses digital PDFs via built-in Mozilla pdf.js and transcribes scanned documents via Tesseract.js OCR completely offline.

6. ⚡ Fast Page Summarization

  • One-click page summaries in Bullet Points, Comprehensive, or TL;DR styles.

7. ✨ In-Page Selection Toolbar

  • Floating quick-action bar on text selection: ✨ Explain, 📄 Summarize, 🌐 Translate, ✍️ Rewrite, and 💬 Ask VRH.

8. ⌨️ Productivity Slash Commands

  • Quick commands: /agent <goal>, /ask <question>, /summarize, /write, and /translate.

9. 🔒 Privacy-First Multi-Provider Freedom

  • Connect to 6+ providers (OpenRouter, Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or 100% offline local Ollama). All keys and chats stay strictly in your browser storage.
